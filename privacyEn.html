Souq Almasharie Platform - Supporting Home-Based Businesses in the UAE

Last Updated: March 15, 2026
Welcome to Souq Almasharie Platform. Your privacy and information security are the foundation of our trust. This policy explains how we handle your data in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

1. Introduction and Scope

At Souq Almasharie, we are committed to protecting the privacy of all platform users, including visitors and project owners. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect the personal information you provide to us or that we collect when you use our online platform.

This policy applies to all platform services, including the website and all subpages, content, and related services. By using the platform, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Project Owners (Sellers) Information:

When registering your project on the platform, we may collect the following information:

  • Identification Data: Project name, project description, emirate, coverage area.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, social media accounts you provide.
  • License Information: License status (if applicable) to demonstrate project credibility.
  • Visual Content: Project and product images you upload.

2.2 Visitors and Product Seekers Information:

  • Browsing Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages you visit.
  • Search Data: Search terms, products you view.
  • Cookies: Data from cookies and similar tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: Display your projects and products to visitors, and enable communication between sellers and buyers.
  • Platform Improvement: Analyze platform usage to enhance user experience.
  • Communication: Send important updates about the platform, respond to your inquiries.
  • Security and Compliance: Protect the platform from fraud and unauthorized use.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We value your privacy and do not sell your personal information to any third party.

4.1 Public Information (Visible to Everyone):

  • Project name and project images
  • Displayed products
  • Emirate and coverage area
  • Contact information you chose to display

4.2 Limited Sharing with Trusted Parties:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with analytics companies (such as Google Analytics).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by UAE law.

5. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

According to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your information from the platform.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via email: abdulaziz1076@gmail.com

6. Information Security and Protection

We take appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. These measures include:

  • Encryption of communications via SSL/TLS.
  • Storing data on secure servers with access controls.
  • Strict restrictions on who can access personal data.
